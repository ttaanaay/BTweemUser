Privacy Policy for BTween

Last updated: July 2026

BTween ("we", "our", "the app") is a quote-sharing social app. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address, a username, and a display name. If you sign in with Google, Facebook, or Microsoft, we receive your name and email address from that provider in order to create your account - we do not receive your password for those services.

We store the quotes you create, your likes, and your follow relationships with other users, in order to provide the core features of the app.

How we use your information

We use your information solely to operate the app: to authenticate you, to display your public profile and quotes to other users (only for content you mark public), and to personalize your feed.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except as required to operate the service (for example, our hosting and database providers), or as required by law.

Data retention and deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below.
